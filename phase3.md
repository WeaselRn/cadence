PHASE 8 — PERSONAL BASELINE & DEVIATION ENGINE
Implement ONLY Phase 8 after Phase 7 is complete.
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
Compare each new valid assessment with the user's own historical baseline. Do not use population norms.
BASELINE
Use previous valid completed assessments to establish the personal baseline. The PRD recommends at least 3 valid walks. The current assessment must never be included in the baseline against which it is compared.
BASELINED VALUES
Use the final Mobility Stability Score and valid physical metrics supported by the PRD. Missing metrics remain missing.
STATES
Before enough data: Building your personal baseline. After establishment: Stable or Change Detected. Persistent Change is reserved for the configured consecutive-deviation rule; default MVP behavior is 3 consecutive substantial deviations.
LANGUAGE
Use neutral movement language. Stable: Your current mobility is consistent with your established baseline. Change Detected: Your recent mobility differs from your usual pattern. Persistent Change: Your mobility has shown a persistent change from your usual pattern.
VERIFICATION
Test baseline establishment, current-assessment exclusion, stable comparison, changed comparison, missing metrics, zero-standard-deviation handling, consecutive deviations, and recovery/reset behavior.
